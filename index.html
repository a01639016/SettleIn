Questions, answered

What students need to know before joining.

SettleIn is designed for incoming and recently arrived international university students, especially during the first two months in a new city.

Is SettleIn only for students who have not arrived yet?
No. The core experience covers both pre-arrival preparation and the first 0–2 months after arrival, when housing, paperwork, essentials, and local routines are still being resolved.
How is the information different from blogs or social media?
SettleIn organizes guidance by city, timing, and task. The aim is to reduce contradictory advice by prioritizing pre-verified sources and presenting the next action—not another long list of links.
What topics will the app cover?
The initial scope centers on housing, administrative steps, first-week purchases, transport, banking, healthcare access, local culture, and practical city survival guidance.
Why start with a survival pack?
It is a fast way to test whether students find the content genuinely useful before the full product is built. Feedback on the pack can reveal which city, timing, and topic should be prioritized first.
