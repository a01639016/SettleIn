EU/EEA citizens

If you meet the right-of-residence requirements as a student, you do not apply for a residence permit.

Check your rights ↗
2

Outside the EU/EEA

For studies lasting more than three months, you normally need a permit granted before travelling to Sweden.

View requirements ↗
3

Length of stay

For stays of at least one year, check the Population Register rules. A coordination number may apply to shorter stays.

Go to Skatteverket ↗
4

After you arrive

Report your address when required, activate your insurance, get a SIM card and check student public-transport eligibility.
